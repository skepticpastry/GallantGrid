Gallant Grid – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Skeptic Pastry ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Gallant Grid ("the Game"). We designed the Game to collect as little information as possible. We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your data.

1. Playing as a guest

You can play the full Game as a guest without creating an account or signing in. Guest progress is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

2. Optional Google sign-in and cloud saves

If you choose to sign in with Google, the Game uses Google's sign-in and Google Drive services to store your save data in your own Google Drive, so you can restore progress on another device. This save data contains only game progress (units, items, settings and similar game state).

  • Your save files are stored in your Google account, not on our servers. We cannot access them.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept on your device to keep you signed in. They are not shared with us.
  • You can sign out at any time in the Game's Settings. You can delete the Game's Drive data from your Google Drive settings.

3. Google Play Games Services

On Android, the Game may connect to Google Play Games Services, which can provide your Play Games player ID and display name to the Game on your device. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

The Game may schedule local reminder notifications on your device (for example, daily rewards). These are created on the device and do not send data to us. You can turn them off in the Game or in your device settings.

5. In-app purchases and ads

The Game may offer in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. If advertising is added to the Game in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the ad provider and the data it uses before ads are enabled.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any analytics or tracking data.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Data handled by Google (sign-in, Drive, Play Games, Play Billing) is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data on our servers, you control your data directly: uninstall the Game to delete local saves, and remove the Game's data from your Google account to delete cloud saves. If you have questions or requests, contact us below.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
